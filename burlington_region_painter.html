Burlington region painter

Paint region IDs on a second layer. Only pixels within the allowed distance of a street can be shaded.

Tool

Regions

Allowed zone

Distance is Euclidean in pixels (1 px = 10 m). Existing paint outside a new, smaller zone is kept until you clear or repaint it.

View

Save / load

Export writes a 938 x 759 array of region IDs (0 = unshaded) plus a regions JSON with names and colors. Save project keeps everything so you can keep editing later.

Controls

Paint: left drag. Pan: right/middle drag or Space+drag. Zoom: wheel. 1-9 pick region, B L E tools, Ctrl+Z/Y undo/redo.
